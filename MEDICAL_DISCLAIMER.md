# Medical Disclaimer

⚠️ **This is a portfolio demonstration project and is NOT a substitute for
professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.**

Always seek the advice of a qualified healthcare provider with any questions
regarding a medical condition. Never disregard professional medical advice or
delay in seeking it because of something generated by this application.

If you think you may have a medical emergency, call your doctor or 911
immediately.

This project is built for educational and demonstration purposes only. The
medical information provided by this system is sourced from publicly available
clinical guidelines and is intended to demonstrate retrieval-augmented
generation (RAG) architecture, not to provide actual medical care.
